Frequently Asked Questions

Yes, Gorontalo is generally very safe for tourists. The locals are known for being exceptionally friendly, welcoming, and helpful towards visitors. As always, practice standard travel safety precautions.

We do not recommend drinking tap water directly. It is best to stick to bottled water or filtered water, which is widely available in hotels, restaurants, and convenience stores.

Gorontalo has a tropical warm climate year-round. Expect humid and sunny days with average temperatures around 26°C - 32°C. Light, breathable clothing is highly recommended.

Basic English is spoken in main tourism areas, hotels, and by tour guides. However, learning a few basic phrases in Bahasa Indonesia can be very helpful and appreciated by locals.

RSUD Prof. Dr. H. Aloei Saboe is the main public hospital equipped to handle general emergencies. We also have private clinics available in the city center.

Both are accepted. Cards and QRIS (digital payments) are common in modern cafes, hotels, and supermarkets. However, it is essential to carry cash (Indonesian Rupiah) for local markets, small vendors, and rural areas.

Yes, free Wi-Fi is widely available at most hotels, cafes, and restaurants in the city. 4G/5G mobile networks also cover most of the region perfectly.
